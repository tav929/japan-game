Today's Goal

Know what to say when you need
HELP in Japan!

These phrases are your POWER! 💪
たすけて！
Tasukete!
(tah-soo-keh-teh)
Help!
🆘

When to say "Tasukete!" 🆘

✅ Lost and can't find your family
✅ Someone is hurt
✅ Real emergency only!
⚠️ This is for SERIOUS help only!
わかりません
Wakarimasen
(wah-kah-ree-mah-sen)
I don't understand
❓

Say "Wakarimasen" Confidently! 💪

NOT embarrassing!

Say it with a smile 😊
Polite and respectful
Even adults use this in Japan!

It's okay not to understand everything!
ゆっくりおねがいします
Yukkuri onegaishimasu
(yoo-koo-ree oh-neh-guy-shee-mas)
Slowly please
🐢
もういちど
Mou ichido
(moh ee-chee-doh)
One more time
🔄
えいごができますか？
Eigo ga dekimasu ka?
(eh-go gah deh-kee-mas kah)
Can you speak English?
🗣️

🎭 Role Play: Lost Tourist Scenario

Scenario:
Teacher speaks rapid Japanese.
You need to stop them and ask for help!

Goal:
Use all 5 emergency phrases!

🎬 The Dialogue

Teacher: *speaks rapid Japanese*
You: Sumimasen! Wakarimasen!
Teacher: *keeps going fast*
You: Yukkuri onegaishimasu!
Teacher: *slower, still Japanese*
You: Mou ichido!
Teacher: *repeats*
You: Eigo ga dekimasu ka?
Teacher: Yes! How can I help?

🇯🇵 Culture Bite: Help in Japan

  • Japanese people LOVE helping tourists!
  • Don't be shy - asking for help is polite, not rude
  • Many young Japanese study English and want to practice
  • Police boxes (koban) are everywhere - safe helpers! 👮
  • Train station staff are SUPER helpful
🏘️

Did You Know?

Koban (Police Boxes)

Small police stations on almost every corner!
Officers help with directions

They're your friends! 👮
