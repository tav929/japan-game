🦌 Welcome to Nara!

Nara Deer Park
is one of the most AMAZING places in Japan!

Over 1,000 deer roam FREE
in the park and city streets!

You can feed them and take photos! 📸

🙇 The AMAZING Deer Bow!

Nara deer are TRAINED to BOW!

Before you feed them, they will:
1️⃣ Lower their head
2️⃣ Bow to you
3️⃣ Wait for crackers

You should bow BACK!
It's like saying "please" and "thank you"

🍘 Shika Senbei (Deer Crackers)

Special crackers made JUST for deer!

Where to get them:
Look for vendors with signs
They cost about ¥200 (stack of 10)

Say:
"Shika senbei kudasai"

Hold them UP HIGH so deer don't grab!

⚠️ Important Safety Tips!

Deer are friendly BUT can be pushy!

They might:
🦌 Nudge you with their nose
🦌 Try to eat crackers from your hand fast
🦌 Follow you around
🦌 Nibble your clothes/bag

If they get too pushy, say:
"Abunai!" or "Yukkuri!"
and hold crackers up high!

⛩️ Sacred Messengers

In Japanese culture, Nara deer are
sacred messengers of the gods!

They are protected and respected.

That's why they can roam FREE
all over the city!

Always be gentle and kind to them!

🎭 Role Play Time!

Scene: Nara Deer Park
You're at Nara Park and want to feed the deer.
Let's practice the whole experience!
STEP 1: Buy deer crackers
You: Approach vendor
You: "Shika senbei kudasai"
Vendor: *Hands you crackers*
You: "Arigatou!"
STEP 2: Meet the deer
Deer: *Approaches you* 🦌
You: "Kawaii!" 😍
Deer: *Bows head to you*
You: *Bow back!*
You: *Give one cracker*
STEP 3: Deer gets pushy!
Deer: *Nudges you, wants more!*
You: "Yukkuri!" (slowly!)
You: *Hold crackers UP HIGH*
Deer: *Calms down, bows again*
You: *Give another cracker* 🍘
