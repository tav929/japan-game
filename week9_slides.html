Today's Learning Goal 🎯

Theme park specific phrases

Get ready for the HAPPIEST place
in Japan! 🎢✨

Phrase 1 📢

Narabitai desu
ならびたいです
(nah-rah-bee-tie des)
I want to line up / join the queue

Phrase 2 📢

Shashin onegaishimasu
しゃしんおねがいします
(shah-shin oh-neh-guy-shee-mas)
Photo please

Phrase 3 (Review) 📢

Kore kudasai
これください
(koh-reh koo-dah-sigh)
This please
(For souvenirs!)

Phrase 4 (Review) 📢

Doko desu ka?
どこですか?
(doh-koh des kah)
Where is?
(Point at map + say this!)

🏰 Tokyo Disneyland

First Disney park outside USA!

Opened in 1983
Everything is in JAPANESE!

But Cast Members (staff) are
SUPER helpful and friendly!

You're going to LOVE it! ✨

👔 Cast Members (キャストメンバー)

All Disney staff are called "Cast Members"

They are EXTREMELY polite and helpful!

Many speak some English
But they LOVE when you try Japanese!

Always say "Arigatou gozaimasu!"

🚶‍♀️ Japanese Queuing is SERIOUS!

At Disneyland, people wait in line for:
🎢 Rides (sometimes 2-3 hours!)
🍿 Popcorn buckets
🎪 Parades (sit on ground for hours!)
📸 Character photos

NEVER cut in line!
Japanese people are very serious about fair queuing!

🍿 Popcorn Bucket Mania!

Tokyo Disney is FAMOUS for popcorn!

Collectible buckets with characters
Special flavors: Honey, Curry, Soy Sauce!
People collect them!

You might want one! 🍿
Say "Kore kudasai" when you order!

🎪 Parade & Show Culture

People arrive HOURS early for parades!

You'll see them:
🧘 Sitting on tarps on the ground
📱 Reading, waiting patiently
🍱 Eating snacks

They reserve their spot!
Don't walk in front of people sitting!

🎭 Role Play Time!

Scene: Full Disneyland Day!
You're spending a magical day at Tokyo Disneyland.
Let's practice the whole experience!
STEP 1: Arrive at ride
You: Approach Cast Member at entrance
You: "Narabitai desu"
Cast Member: *Points to line entrance*
You: "Arigatou!"
STEP 2: After the ride
You: See Mickey Mouse!
You: Approach Cast Member near Mickey
You: "Shashin onegaishimasu"
Cast Member: *Takes your camera*
Mickey: *Poses with you!* 📸
You: "Arigatou gozaimasu!"
STEP 3: Souvenir shopping
You: Browse souvenir shop
You: Point at Mickey ears
You: "Kore kudasai"
Cast Member: "Hai!" *Gets Mickey ears*
You: "Arigatou gozaimasu!"
