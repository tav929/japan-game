🎁 What is Omiyage?

Omiyage = Souvenirs/Gifts

In Japan, you MUST bring back gifts!
For friends, classmates, coworkers
Usually food (especially snacks!)
Shows you thought of them 💝
いくらですか？
Ikura desu ka?
(ee-koo-rah des kah)
How much is this?
💴
(You learned this in Week 6! Review time!)
これとこれとこれください
Kore to kore to kore kudasai
(koh-reh toh koh-reh toh koh-reh koo-dah-sigh)
This and this and this please
👉👉👉

Let's Practice Pointing! 👉

Point at things as you say:

"Kore to kore kudasai" = 2 items
"Kore to kore to kore kudasai" = 3 items
"Kore to kore to kore to kore kudasai" = 4 items!

Keep adding "to kore" for more items!

🎬 Shopping Dialogue

You: Kore wa ikura desu ka? (point at item)
Shopkeeper: 800 yen desu
You: Takai! (or) Yasui!
You: (After choosing...) Kore to kore to kore kudasai
Shopkeeper: (calculates total)
You: Arigatou gozaimasu!

🇯🇵 Omiyage Culture

  • MUST bring gifts for everyone back home!
  • Kit Kats are #1 omiyage (Japan has 300+ flavors!) 🍫
  • Individually wrapped snacks are perfect
  • Buy at airports, train stations, or tourist shops
  • People will ask "Where's my omiyage?" 😊
