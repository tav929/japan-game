💡 Pronunciation Tips

🎯 The 5 Vowels

A = "AH" (car)
I = "EE" (see)
U = "OO" (boo)
E = "EH" (bed)
O = "OH" (boat)

Common Mistakes

Konnichiwa

WRONG: "Koh-nee-chee-WAH"
RIGHT: "KOHN-nee-chee-wah" (equal stress)

Arigatou

WRONG: "Ah-ree-GAH-toe"
RIGHT: "Ah-ree-gah-TOH"

Desu

WRONG: "Deh-SOO"
RIGHT: "DES" (U is almost silent)
