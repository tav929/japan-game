Konbini are EVERYWHERE in Japan! 🏪

Phrase 1 (Review) 📢

Kore kudasai
これください
(koh-reh koo-dah-sigh)
This please

Phrase 2 📢

Ikura desu ka?
いくらですか?
(ee-koo-rah des kah)
How much?

Phrase 3 (Eco Hero!) 📢

Fukuro wa iranai desu
ふくろはいらないです
(foo-koo-roh wah ee-rah-nigh des)
I don't need a bag

Phrase 4 📢

Hai / Iie
はい / いいえ
(high / ee-eh)
Yes / No

This AND This 👉👉

Want multiple things?

Point and say:
"Kore to kore kudasai"

This AND this please!

🏪 What is a Konbini?

Convenience Store = Konbini

Famous ones in Japan:
🟢 7-Eleven (Seven)
🔵 Lawson
🟠 FamilyMart

Open 24 hours a day!
You'll go there EVERY day!

✨ Why Konbini are MAGICAL

What you can find at konbini:

🍙 Onigiri (rice balls)
🍜 Hot ramen & curry
🍰 Desserts & snacks
🥤 Drinks (hot AND cold)
🚻 Clean bathrooms
🏧 ATMs
📮 Mail services

Families go here almost EVERY day!

🍙 Onigiri (Rice Balls)

Most popular konbini food!

Rice ball wrapped in seaweed
With yummy fillings inside:

🐟 Tuna mayo
🥚 Egg
🐟 Salmon
🍖 Chicken

You MUST try one!

🌍 Be an Eco Hero!

In Japan, plastic bags cost money now.

When cashier asks about a bag:
"Fukuro wa?"

You say:
"Fukuro wa iranai desu"
(I don't need a bag)

You're helping the planet! 🌎
