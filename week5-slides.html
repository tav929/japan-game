Phrase 1 📢

Kore kudasai
これください
(koh-reh koo-dah-sigh)
This please

Phrase 2 📢

Oishii!
おいしい!
(oy-shee)
Delicious!

Phrase 3 📢

Mizu kudasai
みずください
(mee-zoo koo-dah-sigh)
Water please

Phrase 4 📢

Okawari kudasai
おかわりください
(oh-kah-wah-ree koo-dah-sigh)
Refill please / More please

Point + Say 👉

Point at what you want

"Kore kudasai"

Practice pointing with confidence!

🍜 Important Culture Tip!

SLURPING IS POLITE!

In Japan, slurping your noodles
shows the chef you love it! 😋

The LOUDER, the BETTER!

Let's practice slurping!

🎰 Ramen Vending Machines

Many ramen shops have ticket machines!

How it works:
1️⃣ Look at menu on machine (pictures!)
2️⃣ Point + say "Kore kudasai"
3️⃣ Insert money
4️⃣ Get ticket
5️⃣ Give ticket to chef

Then wait for delicious ramen!

🎭 Role Play Time!

Scene: Ramen Shop
You enter a ramen shop with a ticket vending machine.
Let's practice the whole experience!
STEP 1: At the vending machine
You: Look at menu, point at ramen
You: "Kore kudasai"
You: *Insert money, get ticket*
STEP 2: Give ticket to chef
You: *Hand ticket to chef*
Chef: *Brings ramen*
You: "Itadakimasu!"
You: *SLURP loudly!* 🍜
You: "Oishii!" 😋
STEP 3: During the meal
You: "Mizu kudasai" (if thirsty)
Chef: *Brings water*
You: "Arigatou!"
You: *Finish eating*
You: "Gochisousama deshita!"

Game: Wrong Order Challenge! 🎯

Sensei will bring you the WRONG ramen!

Your job:
Politely correct and ask for what you want

Use:
✓ Sumimasen
✓ Kore kudasai (point to correct one)
✓ Arigatou

Ready? Let's play!

🥢 Ramen Shop Manners

Do:
✓ Slurp loudly
✓ Finish your bowl
✓ Say Itadakimasu & Gochisousama
✓ Eat quickly (ramen is fast food!)

Don't:
✗ Talk too much while eating
✗ Use phone at counter
✗ Leave lots of broth
