Today's Goal

Learn polite eating phrases
for Japanese restaurants!
おねがいします
Onegaishimasu
(oh-neh-guy-shee-mas)
Please
🙏
ありがとうございます
Arigatou gozaimasu
(ah-ree-gah-toh go-zigh-mas)
Thank you (polite)
More polite than just "Arigatou"! 🎩

Casual vs. Polite 🎭

Casual (friends/family):
Arigatou 👋

Polite (restaurants/adults):
Arigatou gozaimasu 🙇

Use the polite version in restaurants!
いただきます
Itadakimasu
(ee-tah-dah-kee-mas)
I humbly receive
(before eating)
🙏🍽️

Why "Itadakimasu"? 🙏

It shows gratitude to:

🌾 The farmers who grew the food
🐟 The animals and plants
👨‍🍳 The chef who cooked it
❤️ Everyone who made the meal possible

Say it BEFORE you start eating!

Let's Practice! 🎯

How to say it:

1️⃣ Put hands together (prayer hands) 🙏
2️⃣ Small bow
3️⃣ Say: "Itadakimasu!"

Let's all practice together!
ごちそうさまでした
Gochisousama deshita
(go-chee-soh-sah-mah desh-tah)
Thank you for the meal
(after eating)
😊🍽️

Before & After 🍽️

BEFORE eating:
Itadakimasu! 🙏

AFTER eating:
Gochisousama deshita! 🙏

Every meal in Japan!

🍽️ At a Japanese Restaurant

What you'll see:

🍶 Oshibori (hot towel) - wipe your hands!
🥢 Chopsticks
🍵 Green tea (ocha)
📋 Picture menus

Staff are super polite and helpful!

🧻 Oshibori (Hot Towel)

Restaurants give you a hot towel
when you sit down.

What to do:
1️⃣ Wipe your hands
2️⃣ Fold it nicely
3️⃣ Place to the side

Don't wipe your face with it!
