🍱 Activity 1: Family Restaurant

Duration: 7 minutes

Parent = Restaurant server
Kid = Must order for ENTIRE family!

Parents: Respond only in Japanese! 🍜

Restaurant Role Play Setup 🎭

Kid must say:
• "Konnichiwa!"
• Order items: "Kore kudasai"
• Multiple items: "Kore to kore kudasai"
• "Itadakimasu!" (before eating)
• "Gochisousama!" (after eating)
• "Arigatou gozaimasu!"

Parent responds:
• "Hai, dozo" (Yes, here you go)
• Point at menu items
• Act a bit confused to challenge them!

🚉 Activity 2: Lost & Found

Duration: 8 minutes

Scenario: Kid "loses" parent at train station!
Parent = Station staff

This tests emergency phrases! 🆘

Lost & Found Role Play 🎬

Kid must say:
• "Sumimasen!" (Excuse me!)
• "Tasukete!" (Help!)
• "Wakarimasen!" (I don't understand!)
• "Yukkuri onegaishimasu" (Slowly please)
• "Eigo ga dekimasu ka?" (Can you speak English?)

Parent (station staff):
• Speak pretend Japanese (gibberish!)
• Act confused
• Make kid persist politely
• Eventually help once kid uses all phrases!

🛍️ Activity 3: Shopping Together

Duration: 10 minutes

Parent = Shopkeeper
Kid = Has ¥2000 budget
Must buy omiyage for whole family!

Math + Japanese = Challenge! 💴

Shopping Role Play 🛒

Kid must say:
• "Kore wa ikura desu ka?" (How much?)
• "Takai!" or "Yasui!" (Expensive/Cheap)
• "Kore to kore kudasai" (This and this please)
• Count in Japanese: "Hyaku, gohyaku..."

Parent (shopkeeper):
• Set prices in yen
• Negotiate a bit
• Kid must calculate total
• Stay under ¥2000 budget!

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Tips for Parents

  • Be encouraging but challenging!
  • Let kids struggle a bit - that's learning!
  • Smile and have fun with the role-play
  • Only use Japanese phrases we teach you
  • Celebrate when kids persist and succeed!
  • This shows you what they've learned! 🌟

🇯🇵 What to Expect in Japan (For Parents)

  • Families are welcomed EVERYWHERE!
  • Kids can be a little loud - it's okay!
  • Restaurant & hotel staff will LOVE your kids
  • Japan is EXTREMELY safe for families
  • Many young Japanese speak some English
  • Your kids' Japanese will impress everyone! 😊
