When You Get Change 💵

You buy something for 150 yen
You give 500 yen

Cashier gives you back 350 yen

They say: "Otsuri desu" (Here's your change)
You say: "Arigatou gozaimasu!"

🇯🇵 Japanese Money!

  • Coins: 1, 5, 10, 50, 100, 500 yen
  • Bills: 1000, 5000, 10,000 yen
  • Many places are still CASH ONLY! 💵
  • IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) for trains & konbini
  • Always carry some cash!
