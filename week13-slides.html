🎭 Role Play: Meeting Japanese Family

Scenario:
You're at your Tokyo hotel resort
A Japanese family asks about YOUR family!

Goal:
Introduce everyone using Japanese! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

🎬 The Dialogue

Japanese person: Kazoku desu ka? (Is this your family?)
You: Hai! Kore wa watashi no otousan desu
You: Kore wa watashi no okaasan desu
You: Kore wa watashi no oniisan desu
Japanese person: Suteki na kazoku desu ne! (What a nice family!)
You: Arigatou gozaimasu!

🇯🇵 Family in Japanese Culture

  • Family is SUPER important in Japan!
  • The "o" prefix (otousan, okaasan) shows respect
  • Different words for YOUR family vs someone else's family
  • Multi-generational travel is very common
  • Japanese people love asking about families!
