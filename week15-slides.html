Omikuji - Fortune Papers! 📜

Pay ¥100-200
Draw a stick or shake a box
Get a fortune paper!

Fortunes:
大吉 (Daikichi) = Great luck! 🎉
吉 (Kichi) = Good luck 😊
凶 (Kyou) = Bad luck 😟

If bad: tie it to a tree and leave it there!

Goshuin
(goh-shoo-een)
Temple/Shrine stamp
🖌️

Purification Ritual (Chozuya) 💧

Step 1:
Take ladle with right hand
Rinse left hand
Step 2:
Switch hands
Rinse right hand
Step 3:
Pour water into left palm
Rinse mouth (don't swallow!)
Step 4:
Rinse left hand again
Return ladle upright

How to Pray at a Shrine 🙏

The 2-2-1 Bow:

1. Throw 5 yen coin in box
2. Bow TWICE (deep bow)
3. Clap TWICE (loud!)
4. Make a wish silently
5. Bow ONCE (final bow)

🎭 Role Play: Visiting Senso-ji Temple

Scenario:
You're visiting Tokyo's famous Senso-ji Temple!
Follow all the proper steps!

Goal:
Complete the full temple visit with respect! 🏯

🎬 Your Temple Visit

1. Enter through torii gate (bow!)
2. Purification fountain ritual
3. Approach main hall
4. Throw coin, do 2-2-1 bow
5. Say: "Omikuji kudasai" (fortune please)
6. Get omikuji, read fortune
7. Say: "Goshuin onegaishimasu" (stamp please)

🇯🇵 Temple & Shrine Etiquette

  • Torii gates mark shrine entrances - bow when passing through!
  • Remove hats when entering sacred buildings
  • Photos usually okay outside, ask inside
  • Speak quietly and be respectful
  • Don't touch religious objects unless permitted
  • Many Japanese people visit both temples AND shrines!
