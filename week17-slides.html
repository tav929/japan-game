30 minutes of non-stop simulation! 🚀

🌅 7:00 AM - Wake Up at Hotel

Hotel Staff: "Ohayou gozaimasu!" (Good morning!)

You say:
• "Ohayou gozaimasu!"
• Ask for breakfast location
• Order: "Kore kudasai"
• "Itadakimasu!" → eat → "Gochisousama!"

Start your day politely! 😊

🏪 9:00 AM - Konbini Stop

Quick snack for the train!

You do:
• Pick snacks
• "Kore to kore kudasai"
• "Fukuro wa iranai desu" (eco warrior!)
• Pay with yen
• "Arigatou gozaimasu!"

Be the eco-friendly tourist! 🌱

🚄 10:00 AM - Train to Harajuku

Navigate the train system!

You navigate:
• "Sumimasen, eki wa doko?" (Where's the station?)
• Buy ticket
• Emergency: "Toire wa doko?" (bathroom!)
• Train etiquette: Quiet, no phone calls

Be a respectful rider! 🚇

🍰 11:00 AM - Crepe Stand in Harajuku

Famous Harajuku crepes!

You order:
• Point at crepe: "Kore kudasai"
• "Ikura desu ka?" (How much?)
• Pay
• First bite: "Oishii!" (Delicious!)

Enjoy the sweetness! 😋

👋 12:00 PM - Meet Japanese Kids at Park

Make new friends!

You introduce:
• "Hajimemashite!"
• "Watashi wa ___ desu"
• "___ sai desu" (your age)
• "Issho ni asobou!" (Let's play together!)
• Play and have fun! 🎉

Friendships across languages! 🌏

😰 1:00 PM - Get Lost (OH NO!)

Separated from parents!

You handle it:
• Stay calm
• "Sumimasen!"
• "Tasukete!" (Help!)
• "Wakarimasen" (I don't understand)
• "Eigo ga dekimasu ka?" (English?)
• Problem solved! ✅

You know what to do! 💪

⛩️ 3:00 PM - Temple Visit

Cultural experience!

You do:
• Bow at torii gate
• Purification ritual (wash hands)
• Bow, bow, clap, clap, bow
• "Omikuji kudasai" (fortune please)
• Read your fortune!

Respectful tourist! 🙏

🛍️ 5:00 PM - Souvenir Shopping

Omiyage time!

You shop:
• Budget: ¥2000
• "Kore wa ikura desu ka?"
• "Takai!" or "Yasui!"
• Choose multiple items
• "Kore to kore to kore kudasai"
• Calculate total! 💴

Smart shopper! 🛒

🏨 7:00 PM - Return to Hotel

End of a FULL day!

You say:
• "Tadaima!" (I'm home!)
• Evening check-in at front desk
• Reflect on your day
• "Tanoshikatta!" (It was fun!)
• "Tsukareta..." (I'm tired...)

What a day! 😊
