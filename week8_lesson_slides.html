Today's Learning Goal 🎯

Navigation and emergency phrases

Tokyo trains are BUSY!
Learn how to stay safe! 🚄

Phrase 1 📢

Eki wa doko desu ka?
えきはどこですか?
(eh-kee wah doh-koh des kah)
Where is the station?

Phrase 2 (IMPORTANT!) 📢

Toire wa doko desu ka?
トイレはどこですか?
(toy-reh wah doh-koh des kah)
Where is the bathroom?

Phrase 3 (Review) 📢

Sumimasen
すみません
(soo-mee-mah-sen)
Excuse me

Phrase 4 📢

Daijoubu desu
だいじょうぶです
(dye-joe-boo des)
I'm okay / It's okay

The Power of "Doko" 🗺️

"Doko" = Where

Use it with pointing gesture!

"_____ wa doko desu ka?"

Where is _____?

Tokyo has the WORLD'S BUSIEST
train stations!

Famous stations:
🚉 Shinjuku - Busiest in the world!
🚉 Shibuya - Famous crossing
🚉 Tokyo Station - Central hub

Trains come every 2-3 minutes!

⚠️ If You Get Separated

DON'T PANIC! Here's the plan:

1️⃣ STAY WHERE YOU ARE
2️⃣ Go to the TICKET GATE
3️⃣ Look for STATION STAFF (uniform)
4️⃣ Say: "Sumimasen!"
5️⃣ Parents will find you at ticket gate

Station staff are ALWAYS helpful!

🤫 Train Manners (VERY IMPORTANT!)

On Japanese trains:

DO:
✓ Be QUIET (whisper only)
✓ No phone calls
✓ Stand to the side of doors
✓ Let people OFF before getting ON

DON'T:
✗ Eat or drink (water is okay)
✗ Play videos loudly
✗ Push or run

💺 Priority Seating (Silver Seats)

Special seats for:
👴 Elderly people
🤰 Pregnant women
🩼 People with injuries
👶 Parents with small children

These seats are SILVER color
at the end of each train car.

Unless you need them, don't sit there!
