📝 Pronunciation Tips

  • R sounds: Between English R and L - tap tongue lightly
  • SHI and CHI: NOT "si" or "ti" - say "shee" and "chee"
  • TSU: Like "tsunami" - the "ts" at the start
  • FU: Blow air gently (like blowing out a candle)
  • Vowels NEVER change! A=AH, I=EE, U=OO, E=EH, O=OH
