🇯🇵 Expressing Feelings in Japan

  • Japanese people ARE expressive about positive feelings!
  • "Sugoi" is used ALL THE TIME (say it often!)
  • It's okay to say "tsukareta" - everyone understands travel is exhausting
  • Showing emotion makes you MORE relatable, not less!
  • Kids especially are encouraged to express feelings
💓

Did You Know?

Onomatopoeia!

Japanese has TONS of sound words for feelings!
"Waku waku" = excited heart beating
"Doki doki" = nervous heart pounding
"Pika pika" = shiny and sparkling! ✨
